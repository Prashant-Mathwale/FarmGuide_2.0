Field definitions
model_supported: whether current FarmGuide disease model is intended to include this disease class.
risk_level: advisory relevance level, not probability or prevalence.
temp_min_c/temp_max_c: approximate favorable temperature band for the rule.
humidity_min_pct/humidity_max_pct: approximate favorable relative-humidity band.
rainfall_trigger: qualitative trigger; not a measured rainfall threshold unless explicitly numeric.
leaf_wetness: qualitative leaf-wetness relevance.
verification_status: tells the application whether the record is production-verified.
